Finding Hope Through Soulslike Games

How challenge, perseverance, and overcoming failure can create meaningful experiences for players.

Soulslike games are often known for their difficulty, unforgiving enemies, and the many times players will fail before finally succeeding. However, underneath that difficulty is a constant message of perseverance. For some players going through depression or difficult periods in their lives, the cycle of struggling, learning, and eventually overcoming a challenge can feel surprisingly meaningful. This website explores how Soulslike games use failure, accomplishment, and community to create experiences that can encourage players to keep moving forward.

The Challenge of Soulslike Games

Soulslike games are a genre of action role-playing games known for their challenging combat, powerful enemies, exploration, and the consequences that come with dying. The genre takes inspiration from games such as Demon's Souls and Dark Souls, where players are expected to learn enemy patterns and improve through repeated attempts. While constantly losing progress or dying to the same boss can be frustrating, these challenges are designed to encourage the player to learn from their mistakes rather than immediately succeed.

Many of the games that helped shape the Soulslike genre were developed by FromSoftware, a Japanese game studio known for creating challenging action RPGs. You can learn more about their games and other projects through the Official FromSoftware website.

This cycle of struggling and trying again is part of what makes Soulslike games feel so rewarding. A boss that seems impossible at first can slowly become manageable as the player learns its attacks and becomes more confident. Eventually succeeding after several failed attempts can make even a small amount of progress feel important. For some players, that experience can create a meaningful reminder that failure does not always mean giving up.

Finding Meaning in Failure

Instead of failure being the end, these games expect the player to fail repeatedly. Each attempt can teach you something, and eventually defeating something that once seemed impossible can create a strong sense of accomplishment.

  • Death as a learning experience — Repeated attempts can teach players patience and persistence.
  • Learning from failure — Failure is expected, encouraging players to learn from mistakes and adapt to challenges.
  • Celebrating small victories — Overcoming difficult challenges can create a strong sense of accomplishment.

More Than Just a Difficult Game

Soulslike games are more than just their stereotype of simply “really hard games.” They offer deep storytelling, intricate world-building, and meaningful choices that make the player feel invested in the outcome.

Many Soulslike games take place in dark and seemingly hopeless worlds where the player is surrounded by ruined environments, dangerous enemies, and characters who have lost hope. Even with everything working against them, the player is still encouraged to continue exploring and moving forward. There is almost always another path to take, another place to discover, or another challenge waiting to be overcome. This can make the journey feel meaningful because the game constantly reminds the player that even when progress feels difficult or impossible, there is still a reason to keep going.

The community surrounding Soulslike games can also make the experience feel less lonely. Players often share strategies for difficult bosses, discuss hidden lore, and help each other understand parts of the game they may have missed. Some games even allow players to leave messages behind for strangers or summon other players for help during difficult fights. Even though much of the journey is experienced alone, these small interactions create a sense that other players are going through the same struggles alongside you. Asking for help or learning from someone else can become just as much a part of the experience as overcoming a challenge on your own. This sense of community can help players feel less alone in their struggles and more motivated to keep trying. The shared experience of overcoming challenges together can create a sense of camaraderie and support that extends beyond the game itself.

Persistence

Soulslike games encourage players to keep trying even after repeated failure. Learning from each attempt can turn an impossible challenge into something that eventually feels achievable.

Community Support

Even in games that can feel lonely, players can help one another by sharing strategies, leaving messages, discussing lore, and joining each other during difficult encounters.

Exploration

Exploring ruined worlds can reward curiosity with hidden areas, characters, items, and pieces of the story. Discovering something new gives players another reason to continue moving forward.
